Live Demo · Agentic RAG

An agentic claims analyst that works over live browser-ingested documents.

Instead of a canned answer over a fixed toy knowledge base, this page builds a corpus from portfolio pages and whatever policy notes you paste or upload. The agent plans, chooses tools, calculates delay, runs hybrid retrieval, finds contradictions, critiques evidence gaps, and only then writes a cited memo.
