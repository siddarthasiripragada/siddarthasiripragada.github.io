STAGE 03 · DELTA LAKE ARCHITECTURE

ACID at
Petabyte Scale

Delta Lake brings database-grade reliability to the data lake. Time travel enables instant rollback. Schema evolution removes the fear of change.

WHY DELTA LAKE OVER HIVE

Traditional Hive tables have no ACID guarantees — failed jobs leave partial data, corrupting downstream pipelines. Delta's transaction log is an append-only audit trail, making every operation atomic and auditable. Z-ordering co-locates related data, cutting scan costs by up to 70%.

STAGE 04 · DATA VAULT 2.0 MODELING

Hubs · Links
· Satellites

Data Vault decouples business keys from descriptive attributes. The result: a model that absorbs change without rework, with full historical lineage baked in.

WHY DATA VAULT OVER 3NF / KIMBALL AT STAGING

Traditional staging layers break when source systems change. Data Vault's pattern-driven design means new sources plug in without modifying existing structures. Every row has a load timestamp and record source — making compliance audits trivial.

Data Vault 2.0 Hash Keys Load Timestamps Record Source dbt Snowflake
🔵

HUB

Business keys only. Unique identities. One row per unique entity. Surrogate hash key.

CUSTOMER_HUB

Relationships between hubs. Many-to-many. No attributes. Only foreign hash keys.

ORDER_CUSTOMER_LNK

SATELLITE

CUSTOMER_SAT

This guarantees full audit trail and idempotent loads.

STAGE 05 · DIMENSIONAL MODELING IN SNOWFLAKE

Star Schema
in Snowflake

Kimball-style dimensional modeling delivers sub-second BI performance. SCD Type 2 preserves history on slowly changing dimensions. Facts hold only foreign keys and measures.

WHY STAR SCHEMA OVER NORMALIZED IN SNOWFLAKE

Snowflake's columnar storage and micro-partitioning make wide fact scans extremely cheap. Star schema's denormalization eliminates expensive joins, enabling Power BI DirectQuery to run without data imports. Query times drop from minutes to milliseconds.

STAGE 10 · ORCHESTRATION

ADF + Airflow
= Zero Downtime

Azure Data Factory handles cloud-native data movement. Apache Airflow orchestrates complex ML and transformation DAGs. Together, every pipeline is observable, retryable, and SLA-managed.

ADF vs AIRFLOW — WHY BOTH

ADF excels at cloud-native data movement (copy activity, data flows, IR connectors). Airflow excels at complex dependency graphs with Python logic. Using both gives cloud-native efficiency for ETL and full programmability for ML pipelines.
