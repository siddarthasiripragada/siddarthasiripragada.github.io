About

I design and build production ML systems and enterprise data platforms — from raw ingestion to inference, from dimensional modeling to LLM-powered applications.

Over the past decade I have worked across the full stack of modern data and ML engineering: architecting multi-terabyte analytical platforms on Snowflake and Databricks, building retrieval-augmented generation systems backed by vector databases, fine-tuning transformer models for domain-specific tasks, and designing the data vault and star schema foundations that power enterprise analytics at scale.

My approach is grounded in engineering rigor — correctness and reliability first, then performance. I write systems that are observable, testable, and maintainable by teams beyond their initial author.

Previously: data engineering at financial services and retail enterprise clients across North America and APAC. Currently consulting for organizations modernizing their ML infrastructure and data architecture on AWS and Azure.
